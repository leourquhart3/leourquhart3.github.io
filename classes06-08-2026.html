Beginner Lessons

Our beginner lessons are designed to introduce you to the fundamentals of round dancing in a supportive and fun environment. You'll learn the basic steps, rhythms, and techniques while building confidence and making new friends. Beginner lessons mostly equate to phase 2 & 3; some of the advanced rhythms, such as West Coast Swing, Paso Doble, and Samba begin at phase 4.

Intermediate Lessons

Our intermediate lessons are designed for those who have a basic understanding of round dancing and want to improve their skills. You'll learn more complex steps, rhythms, and techniques while continuing to build confidence and make new friends. Intermediate lessons mostly equate to phase 4, 5, and 6.

Private Lessons

Our private lessons offer personalized instruction for those who want to focus on specific aspects of round dancing. You'll receive one-on-one attention from our experienced instructors to help you achieve your goals.

Dance Teaches

Our dance teaches are designed to teach you current and popular dances being taught around the country. This event is hosted weekly (excluding special events) on Tuesdays and Thursdays, phase 5 and 6 in the morning, and phase 4 and 5 in the afternoon.
