What is Choreographed Ballroom Dancing?

Choreographed Ballroom Dancing is a form of Ballroom Dancing where each piece of music is uniquely choreographed, and the entire dance floor is dancing the same steps at the same time.  Dance routines are cued so that while dancing, one hears the name of the next dance figure just before it needs to be executed, removing the stress of trying to think of what comes next.

Dance routines conform predominately to international ballroom standards and consist of all dance rhythms – Waltz, Foxtrot, Bolero, Rumba, Cha Cha, Tango, West Coast Swing, Jive, Paso Doble, etc. Many routines have been written to all rhythms using ballroom figures of all levels.

Choreographed Ballroom Dancing provides personal, non-competitive enjoyment.  It provides a source of mental and physical exercise, social interaction and personal challenge. The music is exciting and captivating, and the choreography allows one to not only hear, but to experience the music.
